\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Subtitle mask (in preprocessing step).} Removing subtitle suppression causes subtitle regions to be repeatedly included in subsequent detection boxes (Fig.~\ref{fig:abl_vis}\subref{fig:abl_subtitle_rm}). We compare subtitle localization using \textbf{Canny} versus the \textbf{HSV \emph{V}-channel}; Canny produces inferior masks, while the \emph{V}-channel based detection is more reliable in our videos. We also test a band-shaped occlusion mask (\texttt{Y\_band}); however, birds occluded by the subtitle band become undetectable, leading to persistent false negatives.

    \item \textbf{Specular highlight mask (\texttt{spec\_mask}, in preprocessing step).} Disabling \texttt{spec\_mask} increases false positives, as reflective backgrounds (e.g., water glints and leaf reflections) are more frequently captured by detection boxes.

    \item \textbf{Multi-ROI (in camera motion compensation step).} We ablate multi-region ROI selection and also replace it with a simple strip ROI. Both alternatives underperform \textbf{multi-ROI}, indicating that restricting attention to multiple valid regions better suppresses irrelevant areas and reduces spurious detections (Fig.~\ref{fig:abl_vis}\subref{fig:abl_roi_alt}).

    \item \textbf{Frame differencing with KNN (in candiadate generation step).} We discover for some frames there are no difference so there are no candidate, therefore, we want to use KNN subtraction(which can learn foreground based on OpenCV). KNN is more useful in stability background, our background is too dynamic so that it can learn nothing， what's more, in bird's low motion situation(where there are no difference) KNN can't catch the motion neither. We also chang the fusion to an \textbf{OR} rule (foreground if \texttt{Diff} \emph{or} KNN fires replace \emph{and}) leads to severe mask expansion; detection boxes can grow uncontrollably and even cover most of the frame (Fig.~\ref{fig:abl_vis}\subref{fig:abl_or_expand}), suggesting that KNN tends to classify substantial background as foreground under dynamic scenes.

    \item \textbf{Background subtraction: KNN vs.\ MOG2 (in candiadate generation step).} Replacing KNN with \textbf{MOG2} degrades performance in our setting, producing less usable foreground masks for subsequent detection/association (e.g., noisier or less stable segmentation under illumination changes and dynamic backgrounds).
\end{itemize}
